The arc

My career looks like a zigzag — until you see the pattern.

I started as a branding analyst, learning how brands actually behave. Became a graphic designer and built visual discipline. Taught myself to code so I could build what I designed. Specialized in UX/UI to design for real user behavior. Today I lead product strategy as a Sr. Product Manager at Sprout — shipping both web and mobile apps, from first insight to release.

Not a detour — a compounding career. Every role raised the value of the one before it.

Outside the corporate track, I run two businesses of my own: tocoffee, a coffee shop, and Gouw.id, premium local snacks. Real operations, real inventory, real customers. When it's your own money on the shelf, you learn what "user-centric" actually costs.
