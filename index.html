About

🧩 A career built one layer at a time

My career didn't follow a straight line,
and that's something I've come to appreciate.

I started in branding, learning how businesses communicate and how people form perceptions. That curiosity naturally led me into design, where I focused on creating clearer and more meaningful experiences.

As I became more interested in how products were actually built, I taught myself web development so I could better understand the connection between design and technology. Later, I specialized in UX/UI design, combining creativity with user behavior and product thinking.

Today, as a Product Manager, I bring together lessons from each chapter of that journey — working across research, strategy, design, engineering, and business to help teams build products that create real value.
